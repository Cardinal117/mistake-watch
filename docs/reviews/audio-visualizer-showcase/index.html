Best overall

01

The radial aperture feels native to Mistake Watch's Signal Aperture identity. It creates a strong centre without covering artwork or room controls.

Best technical

Mirror Spectrum

02

A clean mirrored FFT view exposes musical structure immediately and degrades to fewer bars on compact devices.

Why test: the lowest-cost animated candidate in the initial local frame-cadence run.

Best fluid replacement

Siri Ribbon

03

Three bounded curves preserve fluid signal movement without the former oversized ribbon surfaces.

Why test: directly replaces Signal Ribbon while supporting deterministic tempo fallback.

Best field experiment

Dot Waves

04

A fixed-density dot field responds to tempo and spectrum energy without viewport-width loops or per-dot DOM animation.

Why test: visually distinct and strictly bounded on both desktop and compact layouts.

Implementation boundary

Prototype first, production second

  • This page samples its own audio through the browser's Web Audio API.
  • Mistake Watch can only do the same for directly playable, CORS-compatible media. YouTube iframe audio needs the existing generated fallback.
  • Production modes should pause off-screen, cap device pixel ratio, reduce FFT size on compact devices, and keep Static Artwork as the reduced-motion fallback.
  • No third-party visualizer library is required for these directions.
