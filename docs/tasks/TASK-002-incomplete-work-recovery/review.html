Three-Task Split

Task 1 TASK-002.8A

Account Identity and Owner Authority

  • Google OAuth with minimal identity scopes only: openid, email, profile.
  • Profiles, RLS, role model, and server-side owner helper.
  • Guest-to-account migration with explicit confirmation.
  • Global account/avatar button and Account Command Panel shell.
  • No YouTube, Drive, friends, history aggregation, upload, or provider recommendations.
Task 2 TASK-002.10

Personalization and First-Party History

  • Durable profile preferences and controlled theme presets.
  • Watch/listen settings that improve repeated use.
  • Saved, recent, and owned rooms.
  • First-party Mistake Watch history for real Most listened, Recently played, profile previews, and recommendation seeds.
  • No friends and no external provider data scopes.
Task 3 TASK-002.10C

Social Graph and Provider Permissions

  • Friend requests only between signed-in accounts.
  • Guests never become durable friend graph nodes.
  • Friend room visibility, invites, notifications, privacy, and blocking.
  • Optional Google/YouTube scopes only through incremental consent for concrete features.
  • First-party history remains the primary recommendation source.

Decisions Locked In This Spec

Guest-first

Guests remain valid room participants. They can create/join rooms, queue/chat/control where permissions allow, and keep temporary identity without signing in.

Account-enhanced

Accounts add durability: profile, preferences, saved rooms, owner authority, first-party history, and later social/provider features.

Minimal Google auth

Initial Google login is identity only. No YouTube, Drive, playlist, history, contacts, calendar, or offline access scopes in TASK-002.8A.

Friends require accounts

Friend relationships require signed-in accounts on both sides. Guests are session participants, not durable social identities.

Recommendations

Mistake Watch first-party history is the default recommendation source. Provider data is optional and scoped later.

Do not collapse roles

App/media owner, room host, signed-in member, and guest identity are separate concepts and must not become one frontend role flag.

Approval Checkpoint

Approving this spec means TASK-002.8A should be implemented as the identity and owner-authority foundation only. Personalization/history and social/provider permissions remain separate follow-up tasks.
